Loop

  1. Collect. Read-only pull of bank payments, unpaid invoices, queued payments and balances.
  2. Mine. Infer each supplier's amount range and timing, with a backtest against history.
  3. Approve. The founder signs off rules. Each change bumps the policy version.
  4. Guard. Every queued bill gets approve, hold or escalate with a reason and evidence.
  5. Attack. The red team writes bad bills that target specific rules.
  6. Patch. Each miss becomes a proposed rule, backtested, approved by a human, then replayed.

Safety design

  • No agent can pay, approve, freeze or edit anything in Seapoint. Release is a human click.
  • Six invariants run in plain code. An agent can make a decision stricter, never looser.
  • Rules change only with founder approval, and every decision records the policy version it used.
  • Patches show their backtest before anyone approves them.
  • Bill text is data. Wording that tries to instruct the reviewer is itself a red flag.
  • Red-team bills never leave memory.
